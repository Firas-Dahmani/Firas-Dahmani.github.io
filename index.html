What broke once, what it cost, what fixed it

What broke

A server setting undid the scoring calibration. The service looked healthy, so nothing flagged it.

3/6

What it cost

In a 14-clip test, 3 of 6 wrong answers were accepted as correct.

What fixed it

I put the right setting back. Wrong answers accepted: 0 of 6. The trade: correct answers accepted went from 8 of 8 to 6 of 8 on the same clips. I wrote that down too.

Another time: a rebuilt image shipped without its model files. Pronunciation scoring failed for every language for about a day while the health check stayed green. Now the deploy gate waits until the models are loaded, and a failed deploy rolls back.

Getting it live safely

Every build is tagged with the commit it came from. If the health check fails, the previous version comes back on its own.

At Readdly: every deploy.

Checking that it works

I test with clips that include wrong answers, which should fail. That is how I found the setting that let 3 of 6 through.

At Readdly: pronunciation scoring.

Knowing what it costs

Paid and free models are listed in one place. The engine for each voice job is pinned, and the paid key has no permission for transcription, so a wrong setting fails instead of starting a new bill.
