What's under the hood

One Retell agent

A single-prompt agent with a British voice in en-GB, on a fast model with low temperature. Silence timeout and a 5-minute cap.

Live extraction

An extract-variable tool fills the intake fields during the call, and a code tool issues the reference. No server needed for the demo.

Post-call data

Area, urgency, the other side, a summary and a completeness flag come back as structured fields, ready to push into Clio or any CRM by webhook.

Guardrails

It says it's an AI, never gives advice or quotes fees, sends anyone in danger to 999 and refers areas the firm doesn't cover to the Law Society.
